# QC Agentic Research Brain Positioning

## Intent

Reposition the QC homepage from a finance-adjacent AI analyst service into a specialized research company whose core offer is an agentic research brain. The site must make clear that finance is one important application, not the company category.

## Positioning

### Core category

**Your agentic research brain**

QC builds persistent research systems for professionals, teams, and institutions. Each system is shaped around the user's questions, sources, methods, standards, and output needs.

### Supporting message

From fund managers and finance firms to universities and professional research teams, QC helps people investigate complex information, preserve context, reason with evidence, and produce sharper, more defensible work.

### Audience hierarchy

1. **Finance and investment research** — fund managers, finance firms, family offices, diligence teams.
2. **Universities and students** — institution-wide or cohort-based access that strengthens investigation, source evaluation, and independent reasoning.
3. **Professional research teams** — strategy, competitive intelligence, technical, market, policy, and other research-intensive functions.

Finance should appear as one example in an applications row. It should not be used as the homepage's organizing label or primary promise.

## Homepage structure

1. **Navigation**
   - Keep the compact technical nav, but rename the current finance-coded links to `How it works`, `Who it is for`, and `Custom systems`.
   - Keep `Request access` as the primary action.

2. **Hero**
   - Eyebrow: `Agentic research systems / Built around how you think`
   - Headline: `Your agentic research brain.`
   - Supporting copy: explain that QC learns the user's sources, methods, and standards, then turns fragmented information into insight, briefs, and original work.
   - Replace the finance-shaped coverage pills with research-native signals such as `Investigate`, `Remember`, `Reason`, and `Produce`.
   - Access form heading should invite people to describe the research workflow or institution they want to equip, not configure an AI analyst for an organization.

3. **Research brain capabilities**
   - `Investigates the questions that matter` — gathers and monitors the relevant source universe.
   - `Remembers the context` — maintains durable project knowledge, evidence, and prior work.
   - `Reasons in your method` — applies the standards, frameworks, and review gates that make the work yours.
   - `Produces work you can stand behind` — creates briefs, reports, recurring research, and teaching or learning materials for human review.

4. **Who it is for**
   - Present three equal audience lanes: `Finance & investment research`, `Universities & students`, and `Professional research teams`.
   - Make the descriptions specific without implying that one audience is the default.

5. **Closing CTA**
   - Invite the visitor to bring one research process worth multiplying.
   - Use `Build your research brain` as the action label where it fits, while retaining the existing request-access flow.

6. **Footer**
   - Replace the finance-adjacent research tagline with a broader line such as `Agentic research systems for serious work`.

## Visual direction

Keep the existing dark, technical foundation and cyan signal accent because it already communicates serious systems work. Shift the visual language away from a finance dashboard toward a research instrument: the hero should feel like a live intelligence workspace, with the source-to-brain-to-output relationship made explicit through the existing flow strip and refined labels.

The signature element is the **research loop**: `Questions → Sources → Reasoning → Work`. It should appear as a quiet, legible system diagram rather than decorative tech graphics. Preserve restrained motion, responsive behavior, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.

## Scope

### In scope

- Homepage metadata and title.
- Navigation labels.
- Hero copy, access-form framing, and research signal labels.
- Capabilities section copy and structure.
- Audience section copy and audience grouping.
- Closing CTA and footer tagline.
- Research loop labels and any supporting accessible text.
- Existing tests updated to reflect the new public-facing messaging.

### Out of scope

- New product functionality or backend APIs.
- New audience-specific landing pages.
- Changes to waitlist submission behavior.
- Rebranding the legal entity or changing the existing site domain.

## Acceptance criteria

- A first-time visitor can identify QC as a research-focused company without seeing finance as the category.
- The phrase `your agentic research brain` is the dominant homepage promise.
- Finance, universities, and broader professional research teams are all represented as legitimate audiences.
- No visible homepage copy implies that QC only serves investment or financial research.
- Existing request-access behavior continues to work.
- The app passes the existing test suite and production build.
- The page remains usable on mobile, with keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior intact.
